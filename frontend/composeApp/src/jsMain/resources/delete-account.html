Delete Your BudgeYet Account

Sign in with the email and 6-digit PIN you use in the app to permanently delete your account. This does not require the app to be installed.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, nickname, email) and PIN are permanently removed.
  • If you're the only member of your household, the household, its budget, categories, and transactions are deleted with it.
  • If you share a household with others, you're removed from it and ownership transfers automatically if needed — the household and its shared transaction history remain intact for the remaining members.

This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Permanently delete your account?

This will delete your BudgeYet account and data as described above. This cannot be undone.
